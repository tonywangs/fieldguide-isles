Welcome to the islands

You are the newest keeper. Three small beacons have gone dark. A creature guards each one; win a friendly bout or befriend its guardian to relight it and open the next bridge.

  1. Choose a starter. All three can finish the journey.
  2. Follow the connected paths using the location buttons. No surprise encounters: choose when to approach a creature.
  3. Strike for free. Elemental burst costs 2 energy. Guard restores 2 energy and halves the next hit. You always act first.
  4. Leaf is strong against Tide, Tide against Ember, and Ember against Leaf. Strong bursts deal ×1.5; weak bursts ×0.75. Strikes ignore elements.
  5. Befriend at half health or less (16 HP). It always works. A full party sends new friends to your reserve.
  6. Rest at a shelter for free. Defeat returns you to shelter, fully healed, with your discoveries intact.
  7. Light all three beacons and gather a party of three. Then visit the lighthouse.

Controls: Tab / Shift+Tab to navigate, Enter or Space to activate. All gameplay also works with touch or a mouse. Switching in battle uses a turn. A tired companion is replaced automatically. You can retreat from ordinary bouts; the final encounter ends in victory or safe recovery.

Your journey saves after each action when device storage is available. Export a JSON backup to transfer to another browser. Imported saves replace your journey only after validation. The seed determines enemy actions; it does not lock any paths.
